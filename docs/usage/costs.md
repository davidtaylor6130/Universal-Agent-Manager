# Usage costs

Cost totals and charts combine reported charges with estimates while keeping their provenance clear. Missing prices must retain a visible unpriced-token count, disclose excluded tokens and label the partial total Known cost; unknown charges are not zero-cost usage. Price overrides change estimates without rewriting observed token counts. Cached machine history can preserve known totals while marking collection incomplete, and local or remote records must not be counted twice.
