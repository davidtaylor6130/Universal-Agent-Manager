# Usage history

Historical usage offers Cost and Tokens views for a selected time window, this computer, a specific host or All machines; the machine picker appears only when remote hosts are configured, since with one computer every choice is the same. Provider filters affect the shown totals, chart and model/time breakdown together; changing them must not mix incompatible snapshots. Token categories remain distinct where reported, models and service tiers retain their identity, and the Time view identifies its time basis. Refresh preserves usable prior data while loading and discloses stale, incomplete, unavailable or failed machine collection: the page carries at most one short summary line, and every detail (machine coverage, partial-history and pricing notes, the cost caveat and history diagnostics) sits in an About these figures panel behind the info control beside the Usage heading, which turns amber when something needs attention. A collection failure shows one error notice naming the underlying fault, such as a full disk, with Retry and a dismiss control; dismissing it holds for that machine while the same failure persists, including after Usage is reopened, and a different collection failure, Retry, Refresh usage or a finished collection answering without the failure ends the dismissal. A failure with no figures of its own for the selected machine and period cannot be dismissed, because the notice and its Retry are then the only account of it. Receipt and combined-machine scratch copies a previous run left behind are removed when the next run starts, once they are a day old. Missing sources are not zero usage, duplicate/fork records must not inflate totals, and diagnostics should explain known coverage gaps. Provider totals account for every provider the build includes: providers with usage show tokens and cost, providers fully read with nothing in the range share one No usage in this range line, one still being read or with read errors keeps a row that says so, one whose history cannot be read keeps a Usage history unavailable row, and none of them shows a zero cost. Totals still being collected are marked as figures so far, and amounts awaiting prices read Pricing… rather than claiming no known price. Cost and Tokens share one layout and differ only in their figures, so switching between them keeps the frame still and swaps the values. Each reads top to bottom: a History row naming the machine scope with refresh progress, the update time and Refresh usage; the provider filters; a headline card; a chart card on rounded axis ticks, with a total above each column while they fit, the open bucket labelled Today and so far, a two-column tooltip and a Daily table disclosure that is the Time view and its accessible table equivalent; then one card per provider with usage showing its share, tokens, cost with its basis, one row per model and service tier that opens to its token-type legend, records and Model prices, and a footer of that provider's facts and notes. Model bars share one scale across all cards. On Tokens the headline is total tokens with sessions, cache read, reasoning (part of output) and the busiest bucket with the average over complete buckets, above one token-type bar and legend; the chart stacks tokens by type with providers beside them in the tooltip; cards show their share of tokens and a footer of cache read, output and sessions. On Cost the headline is the total named by its basis (Estimated cost, Reported cost, Reported + estimated, Known cost, or Cost when nothing is priced) with its source and sessions, Provider-reported charges with any estimate beside them when reported charges exist, Unpriced tokens when any are unpriced or nothing is reported, and the busiest bucket by cost with the average, above a cost bar and legend by provider and the estimated cache savings. Prices are per model rather than per token type and reported charges arrive as totals, so the cost bar, chart columns and Daily table split by provider, with token types beside providers in the tooltip; an unpriced provider keeps its Unknown or Pricing… amount but no segment and no share. Cost cards show their share of cost (none when unpriced), model rows show cost with its share of the provider and its basis on a shared dollar scale, and the footer lists reported, estimated and cache-savings amounts with sessions. Days, models and providers whose tokens have no known price read Unknown, never $0.00, and the chart says No priced usage in this range when nothing is priced. Providers still reading, incomplete or unavailable keep a greyed card that says why. Reopening Usage or returning to a period shows the last answer for that machine, period and prices at once while a fresh answer loads in the background; refresh progress sits beside a Refresh control that stays usable in the History row, where the row itself is the progress fill, and a previous period’s figures dim only when the new answer is noticeably slow. Limits is a separate account view: allowance windows never take meaning from the selected period, though each provider row shows its tokens for that period beside the allowance.

To keep background disk work low, figures refresh only while a Usage view is open and lag new activity by up to about 30 seconds for this computer and about 4.5 minutes for another machine or All machines; while figures are held back the view reports that collection is still in progress rather than complete. Reopening the page after a while starts a refresh at once. The [usage engineering reference](../reference/architecture/usage-history.md#accounting-reuse) records the exact intervals.
