# Usage history

Historical usage offers Cost and Tokens views for a selected time window, this computer, a specific host or All machines; the machine picker appears only when remote hosts are configured, since with one computer every choice is the same. Provider filters affect the shown totals, chart and model/time breakdown together; changing them must not mix incompatible snapshots. Token categories remain distinct where reported, models and service tiers retain their identity, and the Time view identifies its time basis. Refresh preserves usable prior data while loading and discloses stale, incomplete, unavailable or failed machine collection: the page carries at most one short summary line, and every detail (machine coverage, partial-history and pricing notes, the cost caveat and history diagnostics) sits in an About these figures panel behind the info control beside the Usage heading, which turns amber when something needs attention. Missing sources are not zero usage, duplicate/fork records must not inflate totals, and diagnostics should explain known coverage gaps. Provider totals account for every provider the build includes: providers with usage show tokens and cost, providers fully read with nothing in the range share one No usage in this range line, one still being read or with read errors keeps a row that says so, one whose history cannot be read keeps a Usage history unavailable row, and none of them shows a zero cost. Totals still being collected are marked as figures so far, and amounts awaiting prices read Pricing… rather than claiming no known price. Tokens reads top to bottom: a History row naming the machine scope with refresh progress, the update time and Refresh usage; the provider filters; a headline of total tokens with sessions, cache read, reasoning (part of output) and the busiest bucket with the average over complete buckets, above one token-type bar and legend; a chart of tokens stacked by token type on rounded axis ticks, with a total above each column while they fit, the open bucket labelled Today and so far, and a tooltip listing types beside providers; then one card per provider with usage showing its share of tokens, tokens, cost with its basis, one row per model and service tier that opens to its legend, records and Model prices, and a footer of cache read, output, sessions and that provider's notes. Model bars share one scale across all cards. The chart's Daily table disclosure is the Time view and its accessible table equivalent, and the old Models/Time switch remains on Cost only. Providers still reading, incomplete or unavailable keep a greyed card that says why. Reopening Usage or returning to a period shows the last answer for that machine, period and prices at once while a fresh answer loads in the background; refresh progress sits beside a Refresh control that stays usable (in the header on Cost, in the History row on Tokens, where the row itself is the progress fill), and a previous period’s figures dim only when the new answer is noticeably slow. Limits is a separate account view: allowance windows never take meaning from the selected period, though each provider row shows its tokens for that period beside the allowance.
