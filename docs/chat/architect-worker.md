# Architect and worker

Architect + worker is UAM-managed stepwise work: plan, execute a bounded step with the chosen worker and review its result before continuing. Worker/reviewer model selections are distinct from a provider's native Plan mode and from AI Review of permission requests. Keep the pair and progress attached to the intended goal, respect configured iteration/token/output limits and pause or stop on failure, uncertainty or the user's instruction. Do not present a planned or reviewed step as completed execution, and do not silently substitute models while active work owns the captured choices.
