# Questions and permissions

Provider questions present the requested choices or supported free-text answers against the correct pending request; permission prompts separately ask whether an action may proceed under the selected safety policy. Do not treat an unanswered question, a closed dialog or expired request as consent. Submitted answers retain their request/chat identity, and a late response must not answer a replacement request or restart unrelated work. Native terminal prompts remain inside the CLI rather than acquiring structured-dialog guarantees.

A question the running turn is blocked on (the provider's own pending user input) replaces the composer body: a Question tab joins the right of the composer tab row, left of any Editing or side-chat tab, and the question, its option rows and any free-text answer use the composer's own frame, while the composer send button submits. Any other pending question, such as an asynchronous UAM control question, floats as its own card above a composer that stays usable, with its own Submit, and collapses to a compact pill. Both forms number the options 1–9 for direct choice, move between rows with ↑ and ↓, and advance or submit with Enter; several questions step with Back and Next. Answering restores the normal composer, and a draft typed before the question remains.
