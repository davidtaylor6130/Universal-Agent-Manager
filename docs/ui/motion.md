# Motion

Motion explains state and continuity: panels, menus, list changes, disclosure and value changes use purposeful, calm transitions without blocking input or causing layout jumps. Motion is physical and logical: nothing pops in or vanishes; elements enter from an origin and leave back toward it, with selection indicators sliding to the clicked item (the settings sidebar being the reference) and reordered lists gliding to their new positions. Pure opacity animation is for special cases only, such as the long-loading skeleton shimmer showing the app is alive. Working/loading state should visibly indicate progress while animation is enabled and settle when work ends; reduced-motion or Off must still communicate that state without requiring movement. Provider fast mode is unrelated to UI motion speed.

Use `--dur-fast` (120 ms), `--dur-base` (180 ms), `--ease` and `--ease-out-soft`; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Appearance → Animation speed selects Default, Fast, Slow or Off through `data-motion` and shared `--dur-*` tokens, persisted as `uam-motion`. New transitions use those tokens rather than fixed timings. Motion Off and the OS reduced-motion preference share one global stop that also settles fixed-duration animations and transitions (spinners and working rings remain visible but still), and fallbacks that keep a still working picture apply to Motion Off as well as the OS preference. The empty-chat composer moves with a transform-based FLIP transition; reduced motion changes position immediately. Shared exit and presence animations change immediately under reduced motion or Motion Off rather than waiting for a 0 ms animation to end, and otherwise stay mounted for the exit length scaled by the selected speed. Usage and Settings leave through this same shared exit presence. The composer's Question, Editing message and side-chat tabs enter and leave with the subagent disclosure motion (a short drop from above with fade, `--dur-enter` in and `--dur-exit` out), matching subagent cards and rows. A blocking question slides into the composer body, and the composer slides back in once it is answered. Panel, menu, settings-selection and list-selection motion animates only transform and opacity: side panels claim or return their space in one step while the panel slides in from, or back under, its rail like a drawer, menus scale in from their anchor and shrink back faster on close, and selected rows change paint only. The one exception is the indeterminate progress band (the ProgressFill sweep on rows and meters): it moves by `background-position` across the full track under a fixed edge mask, so it fades out before the track edges rather than being cut by them, and reduced motion or Motion Off still stop it. Message edit and branch motion keeps the fork or edited message and everything above it still: switching message branches switches at once, then swipes in only the rows below the fork (from the right on next, from the left on previous, about 20 px with a fade on `--dur-base`) and the branch counter digit slides the same way; entering an edit gives the edited message a 1 px `--accent` outline and dims the rows below without moving them, cancelling fades both back, and saving folds the rows below up into the edited message before the new reply enters as usual. Reduced motion and Motion Off switch branches immediately and keep only the still outline and dim. The one running step in a compact work trace keeps its label and preview in the primary text colour; the accent is carried only by a single band behind the text that sweeps across the whole row by transform and opacity, without changing the row's layout or its timeline icon, becomes a still accent wash under Motion Off or reduced motion, and is marked by a system Highlight outline under forced colours. Dragging a sortable row (workspaces in All Chats, and Pinned and Active chats) lifts it under the pointer onto `--surface` with a slight scale, an `--elev-2` shadow and a hairline border while its siblings slide aside by transform on `--dur-base` to open the gap where it will land, with no insertion line; a collection it would join is highlighted instead, release settles the row into its slot, and Escape or a failed save glides everything back. Rows follow the pointer directly under reduced motion and Motion Off, but the gap, settle and return change immediately. Nothing animates while idle.
