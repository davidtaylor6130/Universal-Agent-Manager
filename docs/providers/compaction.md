# Compaction

Compaction reduces the active conversation context while preserving the information needed to continue. UAM shows a distinct compacting animation and elapsed state, followed by a completed or interrupted summary message; verified summary text stays out of ordinary streaming replies and is collapsed when settled. Saving, resuming, branching and switching views must respect the compacted context. Manual compaction is offered only where it can actually run; failed or interrupted compaction must not be shown as completed.
