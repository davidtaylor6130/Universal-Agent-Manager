# Collections

Collections organise references to workspaces and supported resources without becoming a second owner of their files or conversations. Creating, renaming, moving or reordering a collection changes organisation; moving or removing a reference must not delete its underlying target. Preserve identity, order and user-selected grouping, validate current membership during moves and retain prior organisation when saving fails. Removing a workspace/chat itself remains a separate explicit action with its own ownership and cleanup rules.

Collections can contain other collections, shown as an expandable tree in All Chats; each keeps its own order, icon, collapse state and references, and collapsing a parent hides everything inside it. A collection header's count is the workspaces and collections directly inside it, not everything further down. Nesting is at most five levels deep, and native storage and the interface both reject self-parenting, cycles and unknown parents. The collection menu offers **New collection inside**, **Move to collection** (listing only valid targets by path) and, for nested collections, **Move to top level**; dragging a collection header onto another collection nests it, and dropping it outside every collection moves it to the top level. Moving a collection carries its references and child collections with it. Deleting a collection with contents asks first and states how many collections are inside at every level, from its menu or as a one-item selection. **Move contents up** moves its child collections and references to its parent; **Delete all** deletes the collections inside too. A top-level collection has no parent, so Move contents up makes its children top level and leaves its workspaces ungrouped, and its other references, such as desktop apps, are removed and the prompt says so. A top-level collection with no children offers only Delete. A reference the parent already holds is not added twice. In every case the workspaces, chats and files they reference remain. Search and multi-select continue to reach workspaces inside nested collections, and the phone app lists nested collections flat, named by their path.

Implementation note: `resource_collections.json` keeps version 1 with an optional `parentId`; files without it load unchanged as top-level collections, and a damaged parent loads at the top level instead of being dropped. Older builds ignore `parentId` and show nested collections flat; a save from an older build drops the nesting, although every collection and reference survives. The parent-delete choice uses ConfirmDialog directly because it needs a middle action and custom labels that the shared delete confirmation (#633) does not have. Drag-and-drop nesting has a keyboard equivalent through the collection menu rather than a keyboard drag mode.
