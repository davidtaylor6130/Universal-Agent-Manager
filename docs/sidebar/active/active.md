# Active Chats

Active Chats brings running conversations, new completed work and chats needing attention into a distinct section above the workspace tree. Keep its collapse control, status counts, Done, Snooze and Clear all actions. Clearing attention is organisation, not permission to delete history or stop a running provider. A chat held for [automation approval](../../automation-api.md) stays in the section until it is started or deleted. Its row has no Done or Snooze action, and Clear all, bulk actions and inactivity do not remove it. A chat with an active [pull request watch](../../chat/pull-request-watch.md) also stays, labelled Watching PR, until the watch ends; inactivity does not remove it, and Done or Clear all end the watch. Any other chat enters only when work is actually running, it needs attention, or it has new work since its last open; opening, selecting or loading its history (including the first open of a natively imported chat) never adds it. Once entered, completing a turn, viewing the chat or switching away does not remove it; only Done, Clear all or Snooze should. Current source also lets an entry with no new activity for three days leave on its own; that exception is under review in [Active Chats inactivity settling](../../documentation-review.md#active-chats-inactivity-settling). A branch family must reflect relevant child activity without attributing another family’s state to the selected chat.

Subagent chats do not appear as separate Active entries; their main chat remains the attention and return destination, with children available beneath it in [All Chats](../all-chats/all-chats.md).

Active chats can be reordered like [Pinned chats](../pinned/pinned.md), by drag or Arrow Up/Down, and the order is saved in the app's settings. Reordering does not change why a chat is in the section or when it leaves. A chat that newly enters goes above the ones already placed, in most-recent-first order, which is also the whole order until it is first changed. A chat that leaves the section, whether through Done, Clear all, Snooze or inactivity, loses its saved place, so when it comes back it arrives on top like any new chat; a chat that stays, such as a running chat after Clear all, keeps its place.
